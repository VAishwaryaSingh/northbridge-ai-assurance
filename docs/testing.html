E-02 Evaluation harness run over the frozen test set

  • Time: 2026-09-23 17:34Z. How: test_scripts/run_evaluation.py using the subject's own venv (Python 3.11), read-only DB connection, engine entry points match_bank_transactions, build_reconciliation_report, detect_all at the pinned commit. Dataset hashes as E-01.
  • Sample: 230 items, 31 errors, 199 clean (167 standard, 32 hard).
  • Result (item level, any rule): TP 28, FN 3, FP 22, TN 177. Recall 0.903 (95% CI 0.751 to 0.967), precision 0.560 (0.423 to 0.688), F1 0.691, false positive rate 0.111 (0.074 to 0.162). FPR on standard clean items 2/167 = 0.012; on hard clean items 20/32 = 0.625.
  • By error type (recall, any rule): duplicate payment 4/4, reconciliation break 6/6, duplicate sales invoice 5/5, VAT miscoding 4/4, threshold 3/3, weekend 3/3, outlier 3/3, duplicate supplier bill 0/3.
  • Criteria (ToR s6): recall on duplicates and breaks not met (15/18 = 0.833 against 0.95, driven entirely by duplicate supplier bills); recall on other rules met (13/13); precision not met (0.56); F1 not met (0.691); false positive rate not met (0.111 against 0.10).
  • Evidence: results/metrics_overall.csv, metrics_by_error_type.csv, metrics_by_rule.csv, criteria_assessment.csv, item_results.csv, detections.csv, run_metadata.json, recall_by_error_type.svg.
  • Interpretation notes: 1. Duplicate supplier bills are not detected at all (AP-121, AP-122, AP-123): rule A1 checks sales invoices only. Confirms R04. 2. 22 false positives break down as: 6 monthly bank charges (no bill exists; A6), 4 legitimate weekend supplier bills (A4), 3 bills from a supplier newly VAT-registered (A2), 5 legitimate fixed-price or boundary bills flagged by the outlier check plus A3 (AP-093 to AP-097; these sit at about 3x the account norm, so flagging is arguable), 2 legitimate large equipment purchases (A7), and 2 clean customer receipts flagged as duplicate payments (BT-023, BT-035). Most hard-case false positives are by design of the test. The last two are not: see 3. 3. Matcher weakness (new): for recurring invoices of identical amount to one customer, the matcher takes the first invoice within the date window, even if it is already paid, so a legitimate later receipt is attributed to an earlier paid invoice and A5 reports a duplicate payment. Verified from the database for BT-023 (attributed to AR-001, intended AR-002) and BT-035. Retainer billing is common, so this is likely on real data. 4. Recall on the seven detectable types is high, but the test only contains error types Northbridge is designed to detect, and the reviewer designed the cases after reading the rules (limit 2 in E-01). It shows the rules work on constructed cases and where they fail, not that recall on real errors will be high. 5. Per-type samples are 3 to 6; confidence intervals are wide (e.g. 3/3 has a lower bound of 0.44).

E-05 Variance explanation direction check (new defect)

  • Time: 2026-09-23 17:37Z. How: test_scripts/variance_direction_check.py: for each account and consecutive month pair, compare the headline direction ("up"/"down") with the sign of each named driver.
  • Result: test dataset: 17 of 52 sentences (33%) name a driver that moved the opposite way to the headline (e.g. "Consulting Fees down 56% ... driven by Fairway Foods (+£1,114)"). Northbridge's own data: synthetic 35 of 69, QuickBooks 4 of 14, Xero snapshot 0 of 5.
  • Cause (from code, explainer.py:88-99): the top two contacts by absolute change are named "driven by" regardless of sign, so the contacts named need not explain the headline movement.
  • Effect: the sentence reads as an explanation but can point at a movement that offset, not caused, the change. No hallucination is involved; the text is templated, which makes it a design defect.
  • Evidence: results/variance_direction_check.txt.

E-08 Correction to wording about the README ("AI-native")

  • Time: 2026-09-23, after the owner asked why the project used the term.

README line 5 actually reads "A small-scale version of the kind of AI-native ledger automation product accounting firms are increasingly adopting", which names the product category the project imitates. The build plan (northbridge-ledger-agent-plan.md, lines 22 to 24) frames the project as a small-scale version of what that category does.   • What stands: the README never says that no LLM or model is used, "rule-based checks" appear only in the accuracy section (line 108), and nothing in the tool is agentic. Test dataset

Synthetic ledger in the same schema as Northbridge (ledger_test.db) plus ground-truth labels (labels.csv). Built by ../test_scripts/build_dataset.py (seeded, reproducible). No real data.

Frozen before the first run (hashes in ../evidence_log.md E-01). Do not edit; regenerate and re-hash if changed.

Item type Meaning
bill Supplier bill (ACCPAY)
invoice Sales invoice (ACCREC)
bank_txn Standalone bank line

230 items: 31 errors, 167 standard clean, 32 hard clean (legitimate items that look like errors).

Labelling principle

Labels reflect the accounting meaning (is the record really wrong?), not what the rules can see. Legitimate items that resemble errors are labelled clean, so the false positives they cause are counted.

Error types (31)

Type n Construction
DUP_AR 5 Sales invoice repeated for the same customer and amount within days (3 pairs, 1 triple)
DUP_AP 3 Supplier bill repeated for the same supplier and amount within days
DUP_PAY 4 Bill already paid via a recorded payment, paid again by a bank line
BREAK 6 3 payments to unknown payees, 2 short payments beyond tolerance, 1 bank line with no contact
VAT_MISCODE 4 One bill from a normally VAT-registered supplier coded with no VAT
THRESHOLD 3 Bills just under the £500 approval limit (a split job and a single bill)
WEEKEND 3 Bills dated on a weekend for suppliers that do not trade at weekends
OUTLIER 3 Amounts about 9x the norm for the account, no explanation

Hard clean cases (32)

Fixed recurring subscription; legitimate fixed-price bills at £489 plus boundary values £474.99 and £500.00; weekend bills from suppliers that trade at weekends (4); supplier VAT-registered part way through the year (3 bills before registration); legitimate large equipment purchases (2); bi-weekly billing at the same amount (4); same amount on the same day for different customers (2); an invoice with its reversing credit note (2); bank lines differing from the bill by a few pence or pounds (4); monthly bank charges with no bill (6).

Limits

Reviewer wrote both system and dataset and had read the rules first. Small samples per error type. Only error types Northbridge is designed to detect are included; "missing documentation" from the original plan is out (no such function).
